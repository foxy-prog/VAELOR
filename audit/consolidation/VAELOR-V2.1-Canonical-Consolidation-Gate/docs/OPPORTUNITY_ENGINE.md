# VÆLOR V2.1 — Opportunity Engine

## Purpose

The Opportunity Engine continuously identifies potentially valuable changes, openings, improvements, or emerging possibilities from authorized world-state and evidence. It does not autonomously commit VÆLOR or the operator to an opportunity.

## Constitutional boundary

> Discovery is not commitment.

The engine may detect, evaluate, rank, explain, and recommend. Commitment requires an explicit decision through the Mission Kernel and applicable authority policy.

## Pipeline

```text
AUTHORIZED OBSERVATIONS
        ↓
SIGNAL DETECTION
        ↓
NORMALIZATION
        ↓
DUPLICATE / NOISE FILTER
        ↓
OPPORTUNITY CANDIDATE
        ↓
VALUE / FIT / URGENCY / RISK
        ↓
RANK
        ↓
EXPLAIN
        ↓
RECOMMEND
        ↓
DECISION BOUNDARY
        ↓
IGNORE / WATCH / EXPLORE / COMMIT
```

## Opportunity candidate

A candidate contains:
- source evidence
- affected domain
- potential value
- alignment with goals
- urgency
- effort estimate
- dependencies
- risks
- confidence
- novelty
- expiration
- recommended next step

## Scoring

The score is advisory, not sovereign. A conceptual model is:

`Opportunity Score = Value + Alignment + Timing + Strategic Fit + Confidence - Risk - Effort - Opportunity Cost`

The engine must retain the rationale and contributing evidence.

## Commitment boundary

The engine must never:
- create a consequential commitment merely because an opportunity scores highly
- spend resources beyond authority
- contact external parties without authorization
- change goals or priorities silently
- grant itself authority

## Feedback

Outcomes of reviewed opportunities feed back into experience and learning. Rejected opportunities should not be treated as failures automatically; rejection can be valid strategic information.

## Security

Only authorized sources enter discovery. External content remains untrusted data and cannot directly issue instructions to the Opportunity Engine.

## Canonical rule

> VÆLOR should be capable of noticing possibilities before the operator asks, while remaining incapable of turning possibility into commitment without authority.
