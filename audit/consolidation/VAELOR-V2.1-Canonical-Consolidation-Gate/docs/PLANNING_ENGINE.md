# VÆLOR V2.1 — Planning, Opportunity & Initiative Engine

## Purpose

The Planning Engine converts verified objectives and authorized commitments into bounded, dependency-aware plans. It does not create authority.

## Planning hierarchy

```text
GOAL
 ↓
OBJECTIVE
 ↓
INITIATIVE
 ↓
PROJECT
 ↓
MISSION
 ↓
TASK
 ↓
ACTION
```

Strategic planning determines direction. Operational planning determines coordinated execution. Tactical planning determines the next bounded actions.

## Commitment boundary

Discovery is not commitment.

```text
OBSERVE
  ↓
OPPORTUNITY
  ↓
EVALUATE
  ↓
INITIATIVE CANDIDATE
  ↓
RECOMMEND
  ↓
DECISION / POLICY
  ↓
COMMITTED INITIATIVE
  ↓
PLAN
```

VÆLOR may discover and rank opportunities autonomously, but cannot silently turn them into user commitments or consequential missions.

## Planning constraints

Every plan must account for:
- objective alignment
- authority
- capabilities
- dependencies
- resources
- deadlines
- risk
- verification criteria
- failure/recovery options
- opportunity cost
- scope boundaries

## Priority

Priority is a decision aid, not an absolute command:

`Priority = urgency + importance + deadline + dependency impact + strategic value + explicit intent + risk − effort − opportunity cost`

The planner must retain rationale and confidence and surface conflicts rather than hiding them.

## Replanning

A plan must be revisable when:
- assumptions change
- verification fails
- dependencies fail
- deadlines move
- authority changes
- material new evidence appears
- scope drift is detected

Replanning cannot silently expand authority.

## Opportunity Engine

The Opportunity Engine identifies potentially useful opportunities from authorized evidence and world-model changes. Each candidate records:
- source evidence
- relevance
- expected value
- confidence
- strategic alignment
- effort
- risk
- dependencies
- expiry
- recommendation

## Initiative Discovery

Initiative Discovery converts opportunity candidates into structured initiative proposals. Proposals remain uncommitted until explicitly accepted by the user or an applicable pre-authorized policy.

## Planner output

A plan returns:
- objective
- assumptions
- milestones
- dependencies
- tasks/actions
- required capabilities
- authority requirements
- risks
- verification criteria
- recovery paths
- expected completion
- confidence
- unresolved decisions
